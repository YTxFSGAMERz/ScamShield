1. **Screenshot Vision Analyzer**: Upload an SMS, WhatsApp screenshot, fake UPI QR, or bank debit alert for multimodal fraud analysis powered by Gemma. (`scamshield/analyzer.py`, line 41)
2. **Text / SMS Message Scanner**: Paste suspicious SMS, WhatsApp, or email text with pre-filtering regex and AI reasoning. (`scamshield/scanner_text.py`, line 220)
3. **Phishing URL / Link Scanner**: Paste suspicious links to inspect domain entropy, typosquatting homoglyphs, IP hostnames, and known phishing indicators. (`scamshield/scanner_url.py`, line 118)

10. **National Cyber Helpline & Emergency Directory**: Quick-dial access to National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930) and direct fraud reporting contact numbers for 10 major Indian banks. (`scamshield/victim_recovery.py`, line 10)
11. **Golden Hour Victim Recovery Wizard**: 5-step guided recovery checklist with a countdown timer to freeze fraudulent beneficiary accounts within the critical initial window. (`scamshield/victim_recovery.py`, line 62)
12. **Automated Cyber Police FIR Draft Generator**: Auto-generates a ready-to-file formal FIR complaint letter containing incident timeline, financial loss details, and cybercrime portal references. (`scamshield/victim_recovery.py`, line 85)
13. **Security Hygiene Audit & Checklist**: 10-point personal cyber posture scorecard evaluating UPI PIN habits, SIM swap protections, remote desktop apps, and 2FA. (`scamshield/hygiene.py`, line 10)
14. **Personal Scan History**: Local browser-persisted audit log of past scans with risk breakdowns, filtering, and JSON report export without cloud data leakage. (`public/app.js`, line 1335)
15. **Family Alert Center**: One-click broadcast dispatching formatted urgent fraud advisories directly to a Discord webhook, Telegram, or WhatsApp. (`scamshield/alert.py`, line 63)
